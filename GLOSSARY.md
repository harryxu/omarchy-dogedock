# DogeDock

DogeDock is a dock for Omarchy Shell that displays application icons and gives users access to extensions through Dock icons.

## Language

**Dock extension**:
A DogeDock add-on that supplies a Dock icon and defines its state and interactions.
_Avoid_: Omarchy Shell plugin

**Built-in extension**:
A Dock extension distributed with DogeDock and identified by its extension ID.

**External extension**:
A Dock extension distributed independently of DogeDock and identified in the user's settings by its directory.
